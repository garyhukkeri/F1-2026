Hamilton's ceiling
Mathematically: yes, comfortably alive With 399 points still on the table, Hamilton can reach 514 — far above Antonelli's current 156. The real question is the head-to-head swing: Hamilton must out-score Antonelli by a net 42 points across the remaining 15 weekends (about 2.8 per weekend) to move ahead — roughly finishing one place ahead of him on half the Sundays.

Who Hamilton has to beat

For the title Hamilton must finish ahead of everyone, but only Antonelli is currently in front of him. Russell (just 9 behind) is the nearest threat from behind — a run strong enough to catch Antonelli clears the rest automatically.
